How to Play

Construct words using letters from the honeycomb.

  • Words must be at least 4 letters.
  • Words MUST include the center letter.
  • Letters may be used multiple times.
  • The letter S is never in the hive.
  • Only standard English words are accepted; proper nouns, hyphenated words, obscure slang, archaic words, and profanity are excluded.

Scoring:

  • 4-letter words earn 1 point; longer words earn 1 point per letter.
  • Pangrams use all 7 letters and earn a 7-point bonus.

Word ranks:

  • Your rank is based on your score as a percentage of the maximum possible score.
  • Beginner 0%, Good Start 2%, Moving Up 5%, Good 8%, Solid 15%, Nice 25%, Great 40%, Amazing 50%, Genius 70%, Queen Bee 100%.
